🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

What we collect

When you create an account or log in, we collect your email address and a securely hashed version of your password. We also collect the books you add to your shelf, mood tags you assign, and reading notes you write — only to power your personal experience.

What we never do

  • We never sell your personal data to third parties.
  • We never share your reading habits with advertisers.
  • We never use your private notes for any purpose other than displaying them back to you.

Cookies

We use a single session cookie to keep you logged in. We do not use tracking or advertising cookies. You can clear cookies at any time through your browser settings.

Data storage

Your data is stored on secure servers. We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. You may request full deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us at privacy@moodshelf.com.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete any personal data we hold about you. Reach out to us and we'll respond within 7 business days.

Get help

Having trouble signing up, or noticed something not working as expected? We're here to help. Reach out to us at support@moodshelf.com and we'll get back to you as soon as possible.

✨ About The MoodShelf

Est. 2026 · Built for readers

Our story

The MoodShelf began with a simple question: what if your bookshelf knew how you were feeling? We believe the best book isn't always the most popular one — it's the one that meets you exactly where you are.

What we're building

A cozy corner of the internet where readers organise their libraries by mood, not just genre. Whether you're feeling hopeful, heartbroken, curious, or just want to escape — your shelf adapts with you.

Our values

  • Readers first. Every feature exists to make your reading life richer.
  • No ads, ever. Your reading mood is not a data point to be sold.
  • Quiet & intentional. We build slowly and thoughtfully.

Say hello

We're a small, passionate team of readers and builders. We'd love to hear from you at hello@moodshelf.com.
